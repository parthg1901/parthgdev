I'm Parth Gupta. I build agent harnesses: the runtime, tools, context
management and evals that turn a model into something that does real work.

Most of my work sits at the seam between LLM-driven agents and the systems
they're supposed to operate: the agent loop, tool and protocol integrations,
sessions that survive a closed tab, and the boring infrastructure that keeps
an autonomous process from doing something stupid. I care about the parts
that don't show up in demos — tool design, eval, recovery from bad states,
and figuring out where a model should be trusted and where it shouldn't.

Lately that has meant coding agents, including the runtime of one that
designs, writes and tests trading strategies end to end. Before that I spent
a couple of years at hackathons shipping small systems on real infra, from
Solidity syscalls on Filecoin's IPC to Shiami, a multi-agent DeFi assistant.

I write TypeScript, Python and Rust depending on what the problem wants, and
I'm most interested right now in making agent loops cheap, observable and
safe enough to leave running.
